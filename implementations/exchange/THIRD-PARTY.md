Proof stack provenance and licenses are maintained in `proof/manifest.json` and its source folder by the proof coordinator. Profile v2 pins Lean 4.33.1, NanoDa, Lean4Export, RISC Zero guest image and original Ethereum Groth16 verifier.

`npm audit` currently reports advisories in this mixed legacy toolchain (41 findings on 2026-09-09). Lockfile versions were kept reproducible; no blind `audit fix --force` or replacement of original AMM bytecode was performed. The implementation binds local ports only and is not a claim of public-production hardening.
